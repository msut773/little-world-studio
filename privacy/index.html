Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit littleworldsupply.com (the "Site") and how it is used. In short, we don't ask you for personal information, and we don't sell it.

Information we collect

The Site has no accounts, sign-up forms, comment sections, or checkout. We do not ask for your name, email, address, or payment details here. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use it only to reply.

Hosting and server logs

The Site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare automatically processes basic technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the Site, keep it secure, and prevent abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run analytics scripts.

Affiliate links and other websites

Some links on the Site are affiliate links. They lead to retailers and partners such as Amazon, Etsy, and affiliate networks like Rakuten Advertising. When you click one, the retailer or network may set cookies or use similar technology in your browser so it can credit us with a referral if you make a purchase. That information is collected by the retailer or network, not by us, and is covered by their privacy policies. We receive only summary reports, such as the number of clicks and sales. They don't identify you.

Purchases are made on those retailers' own websites (for example Amazon or Etsy), under their privacy policies and terms.

Social media

The Site links to our social media profiles, such as TikTok. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Some of our products are made for children, but the Site is meant for parents and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Since we don't hold personal information about Site visitors, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. If you have contacted us, you may ask us to delete that message.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
