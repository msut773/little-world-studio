The Birdhouse

Claire found some scrap wood in the garage. “I know who needs a house,” she said.

She measured, she sawed, and she turned each little screw. Twist, twist, twist!

Up in the tree, two little birds watched and chirped, as if to say, “Is that for us?”

When the roof was on, Claire smiled. “Every little friend deserves a cozy place to live.”

Try it! Color the birdhouse your favorite colors, then give the two birds names. What would you build for a little friend?
