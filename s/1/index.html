Claire built a little garden box out of wood. She filled it with soil and planted three tiny seeds.

Every morning, she gave them a drink. Sprinkle, sprinkle, sprinkle!

A little bird came to watch. A butterfly floated by. And one sunny day… three flowers popped up to say hello.

“Little things grow when you take care of them,” Claire smiled. “And so do we.”
