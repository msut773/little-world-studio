About us

Little things that make home a little world

Little World Supply is a small, independent shop based in Ohio, USA. We make things for people who like to make, build, and play, and we share the supplies and finds we actually use.

What you'll find here

  • Our own designs. Funny tees, keepsakes, and printable downloads, sold through our Etsy shop. Apparel is printed when you order.
  • Our book. Claire Builds a Little World is a coloring adventure for ages 4–7 about creativity and confidence, with 25 scenes. Each scene has a short read-aloud story on this site.
  • Free printables. Coloring pages you can print at home, free.
  • Claire's Finds. Creative supplies, handy gadgets, and home goods we use and like, gathered in one place.

How we choose what we recommend
